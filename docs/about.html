I’m Gabriella Vozar, a digital media designer and content creator with a BA in Digital Media, Arts, and Technology from Penn State.

Hi — I’m Gabriella.

I’m a recent Penn State graduate with a BA in Digital Media, Arts, and Technology and a minor in Media Production. What drew me to the major is that it goes beyond visual design — it’s also interactive development, media strategy, and building experiences that connect with a real audience.

My work spans social media design for PHA Icemen Hockey, a branded template suite and event photography for Penn State New Kensington Athletics, and short-form video for comedian Lisa Ann “The Glam Ham.” I’m especially drawn to work where creativity meets strategy — designing the visuals, but also thinking through the platform, the message, and the audience.

  • Creative problem-solving across visual design, writing, and code
