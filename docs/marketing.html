Marketing & Content

Real-world social media work for PHA Icemen Hockey — managing the content calendar, designing graphics, and writing posts for Facebook and Instagram.

PHA Icemen Hockey

For the 2025–26 season, I managed social media content for PHA Icemen Hockey. I owned the full workflow: planning the content calendar, designing all graphics from scratch in Canva, writing post copy, and publishing to Facebook — with every post automatically synced to Instagram.

Every graphic shown below was made by me. This was real-audience work with quick turnarounds: game-day hype posts, score updates the same night as games, player spotlights, and season announcements — all designed and written in-house.
